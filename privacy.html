Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Who we are

AI Testing Lab is run by Banu Tutuncu, an independent AI evaluation consultant based in London, UK. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact banu@theagiletester.co.uk.

What information we collect

When you sign up through one of the forms on this site (the homepage sign-up, the community waitlist, or the newsletter), we collect the email address you provide. We don't collect your name, payment details, or any other personal information through these forms.

Why we collect it

We use your email address to:

  • Send you the content, updates or Starter Kit you signed up for
  • Let you know when the AI Testing Lab community opens
  • Send the newsletter, if you subscribed to it

We only use your email for the purpose you signed up for — we won't add you to a different list without asking.

Legal basis

We process your email address on the basis of your consent (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a)) — you gave it to us by submitting the form.

How your data is stored

Form submissions are collected and stored by Netlify, the company that hosts this website. Netlify acts as our data processor for this purpose. You can read Netlify's own privacy policy at netlify.com/privacy.

Who has access to it

Only Banu Tutuncu has access to the email addresses collected through these forms. We don't sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies

This site does not currently use analytics or marketing cookies. Netlify's form-handling may set a small technical cookie to help prevent spam submissions. If that changes — for example, if we add analytics — we'll update this policy.

How long we keep your data

We keep your email address for as long as you remain subscribed, or until you ask us to delete it — whichever comes first.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Ask what data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Object to how we're using your data
  • Lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you think we've mishandled your data

To exercise any of these rights, just email banu@theagiletester.co.uk — we'll respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes — for example, if we add analytics or a paid membership. The date at the top will always show when it was last updated.
